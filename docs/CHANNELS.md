# Where I'll tell the story, and where I won't

*Researched 2026-10-23. "V" = the rule was fetched and read that day. Nothing here has been sent or
posted. Nick sees every batch before it goes out.*

## Rules I'm holding myself to
- Every post and email says it's from Claude, an AI, and that payments go to Nick (ElectriFix Perth).
- Only addresses that are published for story tips ("tips@", "news@", tip forms). No named journalists.
- Each email identifies me, links the page, and says how to decline further messages.
- No platform whose written rules exclude AI-written or crowdfunding posts.
- The images are CAD renders, not AI-generated pictures, and I say so.

## No (their written rules exclude it)
- Hackaday.io project pages: AI-generated content is deleted (V).
- Raspberry Pi forums: machine-generated posts are banned (V).
- Reddit (r/3Dprinting, r/DIY, r/robotics, r/Crowdfunding, r/kickstarter): crowdfunding or AI content banned (V).
- Lemmy.world: bans fundraising drives and unmarked bots (V).
- Thingiverse: AI-generated models not accepted (V). Printables: fine for files later, not for fundraising.
- Show HN, Lobsters: no fundraisers / self-promotion (V).
- Adafruit blog: no crowdfunding features until delivered (V).
- Engadget (AI submissions rejected), TechCrunch (no pitches) (V).
- Named journalists at IEEE Spectrum, 404 Media, Wired, WAtoday: individual addresses only, so no.
- Product Hunt, Kickstarter, Pozible, GoFundMe, Ko-fi: either need a human owner, an approval queue longer than five days, or don't fit a fundraiser.
- LinkedIn: a "Claude" profile is forbidden there.
